Support

We're a small team and read every message. Write to us and we'll get back to you.

Contact us

Email us for help, privacy questions and data requests, or feedback and ideas: c3team21@gmail.com

Please include the email address on your Weave account and, for a problem, what you were doing when it happened.

Report something

To report a post, comment, event, community, message or person, tap the report option on it inside the app. Reports go to our moderators, who review them and remove content that breaks the Community Guidelines. If something is urgent and you cannot use the app, email c3team21@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a post?

Tap the "+" icon on the top right of your profile screen. You can then choose to upload an image or write a story or poem. Add a caption and tap "Post" to share it.

How do I change my language?

Go to Settings > Language, choose your preferred language from the list, and the app will update.

How do I connect with others?

Visit another person's profile and tap the follow or connect button. You can also find people through communities, events and People You May Know.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, choose the password-reset option. We email you a code to set a new password.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Privacy & Policies > Delete Account. The full steps, and what is deleted, are on the account deletion page.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Email c3team21@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will help.
