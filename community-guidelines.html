Community Guidelines

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Weave is a respectful space for celebrating Indian culture. These guidelines keep our community safe and welcoming.

  1. Be Respectful: Treat every member with dignity regardless of background, region, or language.
  2. Share Authentically: Post original or properly attributed content celebrating culture, art, and heritage.
  3. No Hate Speech: Content that demeans any culture, religion, caste, or community is strictly prohibited.
  4. No Spam: Repeated unsolicited posts, fake accounts, or commercial promotions are not allowed.
  5. Protect Privacy: Do not share others' personal information without consent.
  6. Reporting: Use the report button to flag content that violates these guidelines.
  7. Consequences: Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or permanent ban.
