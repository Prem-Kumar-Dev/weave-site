Delete your Weave account

Last updated: 20 October 2026

You can delete your account yourself, inside the app, at any time. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.

How to delete it in the app

  1. Open Weave and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings > Privacy & Policies > Delete Account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, enter your password to confirm it's you, and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm with Delete Everything.

Deletion usually finishes right away. If part of it is still running, it completes within 24 hours.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, profile photo and settings
  • Your posts, comments, likes and bookmarks
  • Your messages. One-to-one chats are removed for both people; in group chats only your messages are removed
  • Events you created, and your likes and RSVPs on other events
  • Your community questions, answers, upvotes and points
  • Communities you created: each passes to another admin if it has one, otherwise it is deleted with its posts and questions
  • Pods you host, and your follows and blocks
  • Every photo, video, voice note and document you uploaded

What we keep

  • None of your content. We keep a record that a deletion was requested, with your account ID and its dates, so we can confirm it completed. That record is deleted after 90 days.
  • Reports you filed about other people's content, without your name, so our moderators can still act on them. They are deleted 1 year after a moderator closes them.
  • A record of moderation actions taken on your content, without your name or account, for 2 years.
  • Copies in our hosting provider's backups for a short time, until they are overwritten.

Can't sign in?

If you can no longer sign in, or you no longer have the app, email c3team21@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it.

If you only use Weave as a guest, there is no account and nothing to delete. See also the Privacy Policy.
