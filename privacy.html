Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what Weave collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Weave does not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, display name, @username, and an account ID we create for you.
  • Profile: profile photo, bio, hometown, home state, languages, cultural interests, and your current city if you add it.
  • Content you create: posts, photos, videos, comments, events (including the venue you pick on the map), community posts, questions and answers, Pods you create, and reports you file.
  • Messages: your direct and group chat messages, including the photos, videos, voice messages and PDFs you send in them.
  • Activity: likes, bookmarks, follows, event joins and endorsements, answer upvotes, Cultural Library reading progress, your last sign-in time, and a log of recent actions we use to limit spam.
  • Device security key: when you sign up, Apple's App Attest creates a key for your device. We store its ID and public key to block fake sign-ups.

2. Location

Weave asks for your location to show nearby events, filter and sort events by distance, and check that you are at an event before you endorse it. Your precise location stays on your phone and is never sent to us. If you tap "detect" next to your current city in Edit Profile, we look up the city name and save only that name to your profile.

3. Microphone and live Pods

Weave asks for your microphone to record voice messages and to let you speak in live Pods. Pod audio is relayed live to the other people in the room and is not recorded.

4. How we use your data

  • To run Weave: create your account, sign you in, send sign-up confirmation and password-reset emails, show your profile and content, and deliver your messages.
  • To personalise Weave: your interests, languages, follows and activity decide the order of your Buzz feed and InTown events, and the people we suggest in People You May Know.
  • To keep Weave safe: we check the text you post against a list of words that aren't allowed, review content that people report, and use the device key and action log to stop fake accounts and spam.

We don't use analytics or crash-reporting tools, and we don't use your data for advertising.

5. Who can see it

Your profile, posts, comments, events, community posts, and answers can be seen by other Weave users. Messages can be seen by the people in that conversation. When you are in the app, people can see that you are online; we don't store this.

6. Who we share it with

We share data only with the services that run Weave for us:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, file storage, and live updates. It stores everything listed in section 1.
  • LiveKit relays live Pod audio. It receives your account ID and, while you speak in a Pod, your voice. Nothing is recorded.
  • Apple checks the App Attest key when you sign up, and Apple Maps turns locations into place names.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

7. Security

Data is encrypted while it travels between the app and our servers. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted, so they are stored on our servers in a form we could access, for example to review a report.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account, except for these records, which are deleted automatically after a set time:

  • Pods: when they end, at most 48 hours after they start.
  • The log of recent actions we use to limit spam: after 30 days.
  • Device security check codes used at sign-up: after 1 day.
  • Reports: 1 year after a moderator closes them. Open reports are kept until they are reviewed.
  • Records of moderation actions, such as removed content or suspensions: after 2 years, or later if the account is still suspended or banned.

When you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile, posts, comments, events, Pods, questions and answers, your likes, follows and other activity, and the files you uploaded. Reports you filed are kept without your name until their time is up. One-to-one chats you were part of are deleted for both people. In group chats, your messages are deleted and you leave the group. Communities you created pass to another admin if they have one, and are deleted otherwise. A record of moderation actions taken on your content is kept without your name or account for the same 2 years. A record that your account was deleted is kept for 90 days. Copies may stay in our hosting provider's backups for a short time until they are overwritten. See how to delete your account.

Drafts, settings, and cached images are stored only on your phone and are removed when you delete the app.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can change your profile at any time in Settings > Edit Profile.
  • You can delete your account and data in Settings > Privacy & Policies > Delete Account.
  • To get a copy of your data, or to ask any question about it, email us at the address below.

10. Children

Weave is for people aged 13 and over. If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we will delete it. If you think your child has signed up, please contact us.

11. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we will update the date at the top.
